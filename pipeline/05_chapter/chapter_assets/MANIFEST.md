## Discrepancies and resolutions (running log)

1. **Table numbering alignment (2026-10-20).** Chapter main-text tables renumbered 1 → 6 with content mapping: 1 = per-cell model sizes; 2 = regime overlap by stage; 3 = adjacent-stage turnover by regime; 4 = between-condition attribution (was Table 5); 5 = developmental attribution (was 5b / Table 6); 6 = clock genes (was Table 7). The Zebrafish1 base-model counts have no chapter table — the file is kept as `table_0_base_model_sizes.csv` to back the §2.4 prose.

2. **Table S2.** Only `table_S2_tierA_findings_final.csv` ships. Two earlier versions renamed with `_superseded_` suffix.

3. **Oxidative phosphorylation subsystem.** Present in the Wang annotation (8 enzyme-subset reps per cell), present in `stats_v7` pre-filter output, but excluded by s80 §1 filter on the loop criterion — 4/8 reps are loop-flagged (max |v| > 100), so `loop_flagged / N_tier2` = 4/3 = 1.33 in every contrast. The loop-fraction denominator is wrong by construction (numerator and denominator are complementary partitions of carry_both, not nested — ratio can exceed 1). Correct denominator would be `N_rep` or `carry_both`; under either, oxphos still fails (ratios 0.50 and 0.57 respectively, both above the 0.30 cutoff). See `table_S9_oxphos_prefilter.csv`. Of 45 §1-filtered subsystems, 3 would be rescued under `loop/N_rep`: Carnitine shuttle (mitochondrial), Exchange/demand reactions, Miscellaneous.

4. **S2 Tier A row count = 50 (F1: 26, F2: 24).** Chapter caption says 50, file has 50. The "38 rows" number in an earlier task brief does not correspond to any file under `grace_v4/`.

5. **Direction-sweep disagreements.** 7 of 50 Tier A rows have per-row `dir` label disagreeing with per-rep majority (`table_S10`). Two clean errors (17/18 margin) are Steroid metabolism at BL_120_vs_LD_120 and D_120_vs_LD_120 — per-rep says LD higher at 120 hpf, not BL/D. Any §4/§6 claim built on the published dir label for these two rows needs to be re-expressed.

6. **Table S5 (decomposition) is intermediate and parked.** 17 of 21 pairs have `UNSOURCED` metric cells; 4 pairs have pre-CFF stale decompositions. Three F1_120 pairs blocked by LD_120 base-model polytopes that failed to round within the §11.22 remediation budget: `BL_120↔LD_120`, `D_120↔LD_120`, `LD_96↔LD_120`.

7. **Chain-file count parked-doc fix.** Pre-extraction = 52 (13 unique corners × 4 chains, not 60). Post-extraction expected = 164 after Grace tarball lands. Parking doc §4a step 2 updated.

8. **Tamai 2004 reference (2026-10-20).** Chapter cites Current Biology 14(3):R104–R105 for the original paper. Crossref authoritatively confirms this is DOI `10.1016/j.cub.2004.01.014`. The companion erratum is DOI `10.1016/j.cub.2004.02.040`, published March 2004 at Current Biology **14(5):446** (single-page). The chapter is correct on the original; add the erratum as a separate entry at 14(5):446. No conflict with Crossref — the two DOIs point at the original and the erratum respectively, with different volumes/pages/issues.

9. **120 hpf envelope read (`table_S12`).** Cholesterol and all 4 sterol derivatives in `rxn_gpr_baked_base.csv` are **not in the 105-metabolite envelope** at any hpf. All 6 arachidonate / eicosanoid substrates in the envelope at 120 hpf show BL ≈ D ≫ LD on the fc column (LD at 40–80% of BL/D). This is a prima facie read that Figure 6's leukotriene finding at 120 hpf is directionally envelope-consistent, while Figure 6's steroid finding cannot be envelope-driven because the sterols are not measured inputs.
